About P.M. Public School

"At P.M. Public School, we believe every child who walks through our gate deserves patient teachers, a safe classroom, and the confidence that comes from being known by name. Our promise to every parent in Jattari is simple: we will care for your child's learning as if it were our own."

— Principal, P.M. Public School

Our Mission

  • A strong foundation in reading, writing and numeracy from Nursery onward
  • Small classrooms with individual attention for every child
  • Discipline and values taught alongside academics
  • Confident preparation for Class IX and the years beyond
